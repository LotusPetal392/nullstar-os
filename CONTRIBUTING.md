# Contributing to NullStar OS

Thank you for considering a contribution to NullStar OS.

## Licensing

By submitting a contribution to this repository, you agree that your
contribution is licensed under the GNU General Public License, version 3 or (at
your option) any later version (`GPL-3.0-or-later`).

You must have the right to contribute every piece of code, documentation,
artwork, data, or other material in your submission. Preserve applicable
copyright and license notices, and identify third-party or adapted material in
the pull request description.

Do not submit code or assets copied from another project unless their license is
compatible with NullStar OS and all required attribution, source, and license
obligations are satisfied.

## Development workflow

Keep pull requests focused, explain what changed and why, and run the checks
documented in [Development](docs/development.md) before requesting review.

AI-assisted contributions are welcome, but contributors remain responsible for
reviewing, testing, understanding, and having the right to submit the resulting
work. Disclose material AI assistance in the pull request when it meaningfully
contributed to the implementation or documentation.
